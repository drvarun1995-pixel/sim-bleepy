# Organisation structure and visa notes

**Important:** This is general guidance only — **not immigration or legal advice.** Confirm with a qualified [immigration adviser](https://www.gov.uk/find-an-immigration-adviser) before registering a business or signing the IASME declaration.

---

## Cyber Essentials does not ask about visa status

Certification is about **IT security controls**. The blocker is **who can legally be the organisation and sign as owner**, not the technical requirements.

---

## You do not need a Ltd company

| Option | Company number (A1.4) | Address (A1.5) |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|
| **Sole trader (SOL)** | `none` | Business/trading address (often home — appears on public CE register) |
| **Limited company (LTD)** | 8-digit Companies House number | Registered address |

---

## Skilled Worker visa — practical impact

On a **Skilled Worker visa** you generally:

- Must prioritise your **sponsored main job**
- **Cannot** usually run Bleepy full-time as sole trader or work full-time for your own company without immigration compliance
- **May** do limited work on your own business (**up to 20 hours/week**, outside main job hours, if supplementary employment rules are met — confirm with adviser)

See: [GOV.UK — Skilled Worker: second job](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/second-job)

## Certification options for Bleepy

| Structure | Who signs CE declaration | Notes |
|-----------|-------------------------|-------|
| **Brother as owner** (sole trader or Ltd director) | Brother | Common if he has full right to run a UK business |
| **Employer-owned project** | Employer authorised person | If sim is an NHS/university project |
| **You as sole trader** | You | Only if immigration adviser confirms this is permitted |
| **Future visa route** | You (later) | Innovator Founder, self-sponsorship, etc. |

---

## Before paying for IASME assessment

1. Decide **legal entity name** for certificate (A1.1)
2. Confirm **signatory** has authority to represent that entity
3. Confirm **immigration compliance** for how Bleepy is operated

---

## Questions to ask an immigration adviser

1. Can I spend [X] hours/week on Bleepy under supplementary employment rules?
2. Can my brother register as sole trader/Ltd owner while I contribute as developer?
3. If Bleepy is linked to my NHS/employer role, who should own the certification?
